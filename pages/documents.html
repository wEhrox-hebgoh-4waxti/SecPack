HOW WE WORK

Practical information for a disciplined procurement process.

SEC PACK uses clear specifications and documented communication to reduce ambiguity between buyers, manufacturers and export partners.

A

Product Specifications

Define dimensions, GSM, substrate, application, packaging and quality requirements before requesting quotations.

B

Technical Documentation

TDS, COA and relevant quality documentation can be requested as part of product evaluation where applicable.

C

Supplier Evaluation

Manufacturer identity, production capability, export experience, references and sample performance should be reviewed together.

D

RFQ Preparation

A precise RFQ reduces ambiguity and makes quotations easier to compare on a like-for-like basis.

E

Quality Review

Material selection should consider the real production process and end-use requirements, not only nominal specifications.

F

Procurement Workflow

Requirement → sourcing → supplier screening → quotation → sample/technical review → commercial coordination.
