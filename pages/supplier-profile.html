SUPPLIER INTELLIGENCE

How SecPack evaluates supply partners

A structured review helps separate technical fit, quality evidence, communication and commercial readiness. Public pages never disclose private supplier identities or negotiated terms.

🏭

Manufacturing capability

Production range, relevant equipment, capacity and ability to meet the requested specification are reviewed.

🔬

Quality evidence

Specifications, TDS, COA, samples and consistency information are considered according to the product.

📦

Supply readiness

Packaging, minimum order quantity, lead time and export documentation are assessed before a supply program.

🤝

Communication

Response quality, technical cooperation and ability to handle practical export requirements are part of the review.

EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

A decision is built from evidence

A

Technical fit

Does the material actually match the application and equipment?

B

Quality consistency

Can the required performance be maintained from batch to batch?

C

Commercial practicality

Can the supplier support the required quantity, packaging, timing and export process?
